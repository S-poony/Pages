3. Description of Service

Pages is a tool that allows users to convert local files (PDF or EPUB) into static websites formatted as digital books.

  • Processing: The conversion process occurs locally on the user's device.
  • Publishing: Users may choose to publish the resulting static website to Cloudflare R2 storage via the Pages interface.
